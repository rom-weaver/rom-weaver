Apply ROM patches

Turn an original ROM and a patch into a game ready to play. All processing happens on your device.

Keep your original ROM. The patched result downloads as a new file.

Before you start

Have these two files ready:

  • The original ROM that the patch expects.
  • The patch you want to apply.
New to patching?Start with the sample files in the tutorial.

Add your files

Open Apply and add your ROM and patch. The app identifies the files and checks whether they match.

Apply and download
